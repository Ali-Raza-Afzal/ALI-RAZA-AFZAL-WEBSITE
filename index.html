About Me

Ali Raza Afzal is a young Pakistani writer, currently pursuing a degree in Data Science. While his academic world revolves around numbers, logic, and technology, his imagination has always found its way toward people, emotions, and stories.

*Dam-e-Waris* is his first novel and his debut as a fiction writer. His writing is rooted in an interest in human relationships, family dynamics, societal expectations, and the emotions that often remain hidden beneath everyday life. For him, writing is a way of looking beyond what people say, exploring what they leave unsaid, and expressing his own thoughts, observations, and experiences.
