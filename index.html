About Me

I write about the quiet corners of family life — the things said at the dinner table, and the things that are never said at all. My work moves between drama, suspense, and quiet romance, always circling back to the people we inherit and the silences we carry forward.

Writing, for me, has always been a way of paying attention. Every story begins with a small, ordinary moment that refuses to let go of me.
